Plex tree anatomy

Size is how hard a node pushes the result. Link thickness repeats it. Colour is the band. Factors show an arrow for the direction of the push. Locked branches are dashed ghosts, never hidden.

Motion

  • Motion answers a click: a node glides to the centre in about 0.65 s, and nothing animates by itself.
  • With reduced motion on, the tree jumps with no glide.
  • Every node is reachable by keyboard, and an outline list carries the same content for phones and screen readers.

  • Your startup will fail.
